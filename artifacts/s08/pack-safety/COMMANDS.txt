Safety policy and limitations:
Optional geometry/LODs are validated once and retained immutable for the session, independent of LRU eviction/cache_clear. activate_packs starts a new snapshot transaction.
New session snapshot ceilings: 240,000 triangles and 32,000,000 encoded payload + canonical manifest bytes across effective optional bindings/LODs; shared files count per binding/LOD. E_PACK_RESOURCE rejects a candidate atomically. Not an exact RSS budget.
pack_bindings() returns fresh sorted metadata dicts: binding, asset, source, pack_id, pack_revision; no core catalogue load. I_BINDING_OVERRIDE records committed conflicts only (latest 256 diagnostic cap).
No texture fields or custom material loading in v1; both reject, not missing-texture support.
Path and symlink checks reject static attacks, symlink loops and unreadable subtrees; these are not a hostile-process sandbox or an openat/dirfd atomic filesystem transaction. Concurrent filesystem replacement during activation is not race-proof.
Cross-pack replacements retain caller-order binding semantics (including shared asset identities); additive identities may not collide with active assets. Within one pack duplicate asset identities reject even on distinct bindings.
