## Review-harness corrections made in this batch (worth carrying forward)

1. **A drive mouth flush with the end of its host loft renders as a plugged socket.** A closed loft ends in
   a flat cap that sits *in front of* anything at its last section, so `changed_pixels = 0` on the rear
   view for four of eight hulls. Fix: the host body ends forward of the mouth, a casing tube bridges the
   gap, and the lip's aft face defines the mesh Z minimum so the envelope span is unchanged. The dark
   collar must be a thin tube whose inner radius clears the disc — a cone or a capped collar hides the
   disc behind its own aft face.
2. **The same defect came back from secondary hardware the first fix never considered** — the syndicate
   convoy's stern crossbeam (whose aft cap spanned the whole stern) and its engine bells (built as capped
   `cylinder`s). Rule: every closed solid ends forward of the disc's face, and the aft-most surface at the
   mouth's x/y is the emitter assembly itself. Build a bell as a hollow `annulus` flare.
3. **Swept wings can continue past the mouth.** The vulture's wingtip thrusters sat between two wings
   whose tips end further aft (z=−11 and z=−12), so both discs were covered by the other wing. Re-seated
   on the lower wing's aft-most tip.
4. **Blender's `.001` duplicate suffix breaks name-based part selection.** A filter on
   `endswith('emitter')` silently drops the second `11 Boom drive emitter` (`.001`); strip
   `re.sub(r'\.\d+$', '', name)` before matching, or a guard reports `2 != 3` instead of naming a part.
5. **The camera's height floor decides the aperture.** The review camera's 60-unit floor means a 38-unit
   escort is seen at 35–50° elevation, not the ~5° requested, so a coaxial drive recess must stay under
   ~0.4 × the disc radius with the aperture at least the disc radius.

The batch sheet's harness also gained per-asset `faction` (the convoys are not `rebel`), and
`write_pending_sidecars.py` now honours a **declared shared seed** (`seed` + `seed_arg` in the spec)
instead of assuming one builder per identifier.

## What the weld pass changed after the first reviewed cut

The interceptor was re-seeded in this revision: one part sat **0.48 units** clear of the spine it was
meant to sit on — invisible at a fighter's framing but a genuine float, and the guard catches it
because it measures the exported mesh rather than the master. `weld_scene()`
(`tools/art/alliance_common.py`) now runs in the shared seed before it declares the mesh, so a part
is grown onto its neighbour when the gap is at most 2.5 units and braced when it is larger; the
fighter's worst part-to-nearest gap is now 0.00 against 0.48 in the reviewed mesh, its envelope and
all 2,214 triangles are unchanged, and the other seven hulls were re-measured clean to the same
0.25-unit standard rather than re-seeded. The remaining masters and hashes in this handoff are the
reviewed ones.
